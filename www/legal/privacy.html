EesyLoad Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026. Operator: App Innovation MC, South Africa. Contact: appinnovationmc@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Phone number for OTP sign-in
  • Name, saved addresses, booking details
  • Precise location while you use the rider or driver app to match trips and show tracking
  • Payment references via Paystack (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Delivery photos and signatures for proof of delivery
  • Device identifiers needed to run the app

Why we use location

Rider: pickup pin and tracking. Driver: show you nearby loads, update customers, and navigate. Background location on the driver app is used only while you are online or on an active job so the customer can see your position. You can go offline to stop background updates.

Sharing

We share trip details with the matched driver or rider. Payments go through Paystack. Maps go through Google. Hosting and database: Supabase. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

Bookings and proof of delivery are kept as required for disputes and tax. You can request account deletion from Profile → Delete account or by emailing appinnovationmc@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, subject to lawful retention.
